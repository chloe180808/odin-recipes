Tomato Salad

Description

A delicious Mediterranean salad with minimal dressing cause the tomatoes are so dang good.

Ingredients

  • 1-2 tomatoes chopped into thin circular slices
  • Equal amount of mozarella chopped into thin circular slices
  • Salt and pepper
  • Basil (or basil-infused olive oil!)
  • Your favourite olive oil (I recommend AOP de Nîmes)

Steps

What counts as a tomato salad is pretty loosely defined. You can really season as you see fit, and include as much or little mozarella as you like. The only essential, really, is a good tomato.

  1. Chop your ingredients (tomatoes, mozarella and basil)
  2. Arrange them in a pretty circle on a plate
  3. Drizzle with balsamic vinegar and olive oil (if using a basil infusion, drizzle this and don't add basil)
  4. Season with salt, pepper and basil to taste
  5. Enjoy!
