Creme de Tapioca

Aka crème de persane

Description

A fresh, creamy dessert with the pleasant chewiness of tapioca and a sweet, custard-like taste.

Ingredients

  • 8 generous tablespoons of Tapioca
  • 500ml milk
  • 8 tablespoons sugar
  • 4 eggs
  • Vanilla
  • (optional) chopped fruit

Steps

This is delicious and unfortunately, not something to make in large quantities cause it's only really good for a day.

  1. Put the milk, tapioca and a pinch of salt into a pan
  2. Bring very gently to a boil
  3. Off the heat, mix in the egg yolks and the vanilla (or whichever flavouring)
  4. Return to the heat and cook gently for 2 minutes, stirring constantly
  5. In a separate bowl, beat the egg whites until stiff, grandually adding the sugar
  6. Gently fold into the cream mixture
  7. Place into the fridge to chill
